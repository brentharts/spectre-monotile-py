\begin{result}[\exact]\label{res:found-pairs}
Let $T_k$ be the number of tiles of the level-$k$ patch and $P_k$ its number of bump-and-dent pairs (two flat vertices meeting alone). All eight non-$\Gamma$ species are geometrically identical for every $k\ge0$ (call this $O_k$, and $\Gamma$ alone $G_k$). Their flat-pair counts $P_k(O)=0,0,6,82,804,7012,58106,\qgWPairSeven,3750568,\dots$ ($k\ge0$) and $P_k(G)=0,0,5,72,705,6136,50789,410368,\dots$ ($k\ge1$, with a $-1$ seed transient at $k=5$) satisfy the exact order-$5$ recurrence with characteristic polynomial $(x-1)(x^2-8x+1)(x^2-4x-1)$. Hence for all $k\ge0$,
\[
P_k(O)=\frac{\mu^k-\mu^{-k}}{\sqrt{15}}-\Bigl(\tfrac14+\tfrac{3\sqrt5}{20}\Bigr)\nu^k-\Bigl(\tfrac14-\tfrac{3\sqrt5}{20}\Bigr)(2-\sqrt5)^k+\tfrac12,
\]
with $\mu=4+\sqrt{15}$ and $\nu=2+\sqrt5=\varphi^3$, and $P_k/T_k\to 2/(5+\sqrt{15})=(5-\sqrt{15})/5=\rho_0$ exactly for every species, with boundary correction of order $(\nu/\mu)^k=\qgWBoundaryRatio^k$.
\end{result}
\emph{Proof.} By induction on $k$, every non-$\Gamma$ rule places seven non-$\Gamma$ children in slots $0,\dots,6$ and $\Gamma$ in slot $7$ under the same slot transforms, so all eight non-$\Gamma$ supertiles coincide as tile sets for all $k\ge0$. Thus $P_{k+1}(O)=7P_k(O)+P_k(G)+J_{k+1}(O)$ and $P_{k+1}(G)=6P_k(O)+P_k(G)+J_{k+1}(G)$, where $J_{k+1}$ counts flat pairs straddling distinct top-level slots. Writing each slot transform symbolically as $\tau_i(x)=R(\omega^{a_i}x+s_i(q))$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ in the four quad vertices $q=(q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)$, the reflection $R=-\overline{(\cdot)}$ cancels identically in every same-level relative transform $\tau_i^{-1}\circ g\circ\tau_j$. Canonicalising modulo the exact degree-$6$ quad-difference kernel $(x^2-1)(x^4-8x^2+1)=0$ closes the reachable seam automaton from the $13$ internal seams of $O$ and $11$ of $G$ on exactly $N=15$ seam states with integer transition matrix $M\in\mathbb{Z}^{15\times15}$. No flat pair is ever created at a triple-supertile junction ($M\mathbf{b}_k=\mathbf{b}_{k+1}$ across all $15$ states). Exact rational Krylov reduction shows $\dim\mathrm{span}\{M^m\mathbf{b}_1\}=4$ with minimal polynomial $(x^2-1)(x^2-4x-1)$; although $10$ of the $15$ individual seam states carry the $(-1)^k$ parity mode, its projection onto both $\mathbf{c}_O$ and $\mathbf{c}_G$ vanishes identically, proving $(x-1)(x^2-4x-1)$ annihilates $J_k(O)$ and $J_k(G)$ for all $k\ge2$. Multiplying by $\det\!\left(xI-\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}7&1\\6&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\right)=x^2-8x+1$ gives the order-$5$ recurrence (\texttt{t9\_pair\_proof.py}). \qed

\subsection{Exact cavity rigidity, absence of small closed chiral flips, and 3D Chair44 comparison}\label{sec:flips-sat}

To eliminate the point-cloud tolerance band (\texttt{IN\_T}/\texttt{OUT\_T}) and the radius-$8.5$ placement cutoff of Section~\ref{sec:flips}, we formulate cavity completion directly in exact integer arithmetic over $\tfrac12\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt3]$ using Stokes directed-edge flux conservation ($\sum_{t\ni e}x_t=1$ on $\partial C$, $\sum_{t\ni e}x_t=\sum_{t\ni\bar e}x_t\le1$ in the interior, plus pairwise interior-overlap exclusion) with two independent solvers: an unrestricted exact MRV boundary-edge search (\emph{Engine~A}, zero model-set hull assumption) and a \textsc{CaDiCaL} 2.1.3 + \texttt{drat-trim} SAT certifier on the index-$36$ sublattice of Result~\ref{res:wada-coset} (\emph{Engine~B}; module \texttt{t9\_cavity\_rigidity.py}).

\begin{result}[\exact; \negative\ for local chiral Pachner moves]\label{res:sat-rigidity}
On a level-$4$ chiral Spectre patch ($4401$ tiles):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \emph{All orientations.} Erasing any single interior tile across all $12$ orientations admits no alternative chiral filling (Engine~A exhaustive \textsc{unsat} and Engine~B \texttt{drat-trim} verified \textsc{unsat}).
\item \emph{No closed chiral flip cluster up to $300$ tiles.} Erasing a simply connected central cavity of $k=300$ tiles (radius $r=27.96$, $|\partial C|=284$, exceeding the radius-$26$ target of Open Problem~14) is exhaustively \textsc{unsat} under Engine~A ($54\,252$ nodes, zero hull assumption), as is a $200$-tile cavity around a Mystic seed ($r=22.80$). Under Engine~B, cavities up to $k=180$ tiles ($r=21.94$, $27\,145$ candidates, $12\,842\,210$ clauses) are formally \texttt{drat-trim} verified \textsc{unsat}.
\item \emph{Chirality is the locking mechanism.} Allowing reflected tiles ($\det=-1$) breaks rigidity immediately: at $k=4$ tiles ($7$ nodes), Engine~A finds an exact $2$-tile closed swap ($\mathrm{rem}=2$, $\mathrm{add}=2$, $(dV,dE,dF)=(0,0,0)$).
\item \emph{Random erasure code.} For random i.i.d.\ tile erasures inside a pinned $160$-tile interior disc across $p\in\{0.05,0.10,0.20,0.30,0.40,0.50,0.65,0.80,1.00\}$, $121$ of $121$ trials ($100.0\%$) recover uniquely, even above 2D site percolation (mean largest erased cluster $126.8$ tiles at $p=0.80$).
\end{enumerate}
\end{result}

Thus the failure of Result~\ref{res:flip-cluster} to find a closed flip cluster was not a box-size artefact: chiral Spectre phason flips do not close into local Pachner-type clusters below $300$ tiles (diameter $>55$), while classical local recoverability (Result~\ref{res:flip-erase}) extends from $1$ tile to macroscopic regions whenever an unpinned exterior boundary is not reached.

\begin{result}[\exact]\label{res:sat-defect}
Relaxing edge-matching clauses on cavities of $k\in\{6,10,16,20\}$ tiles ($r=3.37$ to $7.33$) with a Sinz cardinality counter over unmatched directed edges and requiring at least one non-original chiral tile yields a constant global minimum defect $D_{\min}=20$ (\texttt{drat-trim} verified \textsc{unsat} at $D\le19$ for every $k$), achieved by a localised two-pocket vacancy-dipole ($\mathrm{rem}=2$, $\mathrm{add}=1$, two disconnected defect components of $12$ and $8$ vertices).
\end{result}

\begin{result}[\exact]\label{res:chair44-sat}
On the depth-$3$ three-dimensional Chair44 supertile ($512$ chairs, $3584$ unit cubes), erasing all $225$ strictly interior chairs ($1575$ unit cubes) yields a formally \texttt{drat-trim} verified \textsc{unsat} cavity under the exact $44$-contact atlas ($9290$ candidates, $5\,624\,858$ clauses). Even the bare undecorated $7$-cube chair locks the same $225$-chair interior cavity uniquely (\textsc{unsat}, $1\,137\,804$ clauses): unlike the planar Spectre, where chirality supplies the rigidity, the 3D non-convex L-chair geometry locks interior cavities before decorations are added.
\end{result}

\begin{result}[\exact\ for $N\le28$; \numeric\ for $N\le804$]\label{res:dipole-scale}
Extending the classical ground-state calculation of Result~\ref{res:ham-patch} by OpenMP Gray-code enumeration (\texttt{t9\_dipole\_scaling.py}) reproduces all $N\le14$ values and reaches $N=28$ exact ($f_{\mathrm{unsat}}=0.2091$, unique ground state $\mathrm{deg}=1\bmod\mathbb{Z}_2$ at every size $N=6,\dots,28$). Multi-start simulated annealing with $2$-opt polishing (zero error against exact at $N=14,20,26,28$) gives $f_{\mathrm{unsat}}\le0.2293$ on the full level-$3$ patch ($N=82$) and a thermodynamic plateau $f_{\mathrm{unsat}}\le0.2948$ on the full level-$4$ patch ($N=804$), nearly double the $14$-pair frustration of Result~\ref{res:ham-patch}.
\end{result}
