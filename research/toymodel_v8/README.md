# toymodel_v8: flips of the contact graph

Follows from the contact-graph test of `../../RECONNECTION.md` (a phason flip is a cluster of tiles; in 20 of 30 cases that change the footprint's tile set the contact graph changes topology). The model asks what that does and does not give: an arrow of time, a move set, a link to gravity. Plan: `PLAN.md`. Results: `STAGE0.md` (done).

Stage 0 result: the flip relation is symmetric under reversal, so no arrow follows from the flips alone; the arrow has to come from an initial condition, the inflation direction, or a non-reversible rate (Stages 4 and 5). Nothing here yet connects to quantum gravity.

Stage 3 (move set) was attempted before Stage 1 was finished and is **unresolved** (`STAGE3.md`): the overlaps that marked samples invalid are raster tolerance, but a systematic missing tile (holes) remains, no cluster closed, and flips at step 0.03 involve up to 13 tiles per side. No move classification and no quantum-gravity statement is made.

Stage 7 (`STAGE7.md`, from the qwla repository): the orientation-parity frequencies (5 +- sqrt15)/10 of Batle and Bednorz are reproduced exactly on our patches, and the Mystic-pair window has area 1/(3 + sqrt15) of the single window (Counted, 0.3%). Four hypotheses (parity change per flip, local recoverability, erasure, cohomology) are stated with tests, none run.

Stage 7b (`STAGE7B.md`): changes are uniformly dense (rho^2, 0.028 per tile); the Mystic-pair count changes locally in 9 of 27 area-conserving events (parity is a density, not a local charge); places where 3 or more window pieces meet are not where erasure is harder (no ambiguous erasure in 159 phases); the Z^4 coordinate forms are four non-exact one-forms, the real internal plane has bounded primitives, and no map to the hull's H^1 is claimed.
